One engine. Your hardware. Everything you own.

Coco is not a cloud drive with a search box bolted on. It is an indexing engine that lives on your machine, understands your files, and never needs permission from anyone else to answer you.

A data engine, not a folder

Point it at a directory and it reads what's inside: text extraction, OCR on screenshots and scans, transcripts from audio, embeddings for meaning. Hybrid keyword + vector retrieval answers in milliseconds.

  • Works fully offline — no account, no server
  • Indexes in the background while you work
  • Tells you when it can't read something, instead of hiding it

Your password vault, at home

Logins, keys and recovery codes live in the same encrypted vault as your files — on your devices, under keys only you hold. No vendor to breach, no vault to subpoena, no plaintext anywhere.

  • End-to-end encrypted; keys never leave your hardware
  • Secrets are searchable by what they're for, not just their name
  • Syncs to your other devices — and to nothing else

Searchable across every device

Your laptop, your phone, your desktop — one portfolio. Search from any of them and get hits from all of them, each labelled with the device it came from. Sync is peer-to-peer and end-to-end encrypted; a lost device is ejected cryptographically.

  • Per-device attribution on every result
  • Peer-to-peer fleet sync — no relay holds your data
  • Revoke a device and its access is gone, not just hidden
